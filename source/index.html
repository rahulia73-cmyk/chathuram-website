Meet the pieces

Every piece stands the way it moves.

Our pawn stands on a diagonal base because pawns capture diagonally. Every Chathuram piece follows the same idea: look at the base and you can see how it moves.

Pawn

Steps forward, captures diagonally. A one-way diagonal base.

Knight

Jumps in an L. An L-shaped step for a base.

Bishop

Slides along diagonals. A base sweeping down both diagonals.

Rook

Moves in straight lines. A straight, square-edged base.

Queen

Straight and diagonal. A flat base with diagonal wings.

King

One step, any direction. A small, square base.
