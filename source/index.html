Meet the pieces

Meet the whole team.

Reach the last square and a pawn can become a knight, a bishop, a rook or a queen. Here is how each of them moves.

Pawn

Steps forward one square and captures diagonally.

Knight

Jumps in an L shape, even over other pieces.

Bishop

Slides any distance along the diagonals.

Rook

Moves any distance in straight lines.

Queen

Moves any distance, straight or diagonal.
