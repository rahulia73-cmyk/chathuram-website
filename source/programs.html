Level 1

Beginner

Ages 5+ · no experience needed

For complete beginners and children just discovering the game. Students learn by playing, with puzzles and mini-games that keep classes fun.

  • How each piece moves, special moves and the rules of the game
  • Check, checkmate and the basic mating patterns
  • Simple tactics: forks, pins and capturing safely
  • Playing complete games and writing down moves
Level 2

Intermediate

Knows the rules · plays full games

For students who play full games and want to stop losing pieces and start winning them.

  • Tactical patterns and calculation
  • Opening principles and a first set of openings
  • Essential endgames: king and pawn, rook endings
  • Practice games with review of mistakes
Level 3

Advanced

Club and rated players

For players competing in tournaments who want to build a deeper understanding of the game.

  • Positional play, pawn structures and planning
  • Building and preparing an opening repertoire
  • Complex endgames and technique
  • Analysis of students' own tournament games
1-on-1

Private coaching

Any level · in person or online

Individual sessions with a plan built for one student. Good for focused tournament preparation, working on a specific weakness, or when group timings don't fit.

  • Personal assessment and training plan
  • Preparation for upcoming tournaments
  • Flexible scheduling

In person, Hyderabad

Classes at our Hyderabad centre, with real boards, clocks and over-the-board practice games.

Live online

Live interactive classes on a shared online board. Join from anywhere in India or abroad.
