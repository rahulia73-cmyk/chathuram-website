Level 01

Beginner Level

How the pieces move, the rules of the game and the basic checkmates.

  • How each piece moves, castling, en passant and promotion
  • Check, checkmate and stalemate
  • Basic checkmates with queen and rook
  • Playing complete games
Level 02

Advanced Beginner

First tactics, opening principles and winning material safely.

  • Forks, pins, skewers and discovered attacks
  • Opening principles: centre, development, king safety
  • Counting material and avoiding blunders
  • Mate-in-one and mate-in-two puzzles
Level 03

Intermediate Level 1

Combinations, calculation and the essential pawn endgames.

  • Combining tactics and calculating short lines
  • Attacking the king
  • King and pawn endgames: opposition and the square
  • Reviewing your own games
Level 04

Intermediate Level 2

A first opening repertoire, middlegame plans and rook endgames.

  • Building a first opening repertoire with White and Black
  • Finding a plan in the middlegame
  • Essential rook endgames
  • Practice games with analysis
Level 05

Advanced Level 1

Positional play, pawn structures and strategic planning.

  • Pawn structures and the plans that go with them
  • Good and bad pieces, weak squares, open files
  • Prophylactic thinking
  • Deeper calculation
Level 06

Advanced Level 2

Deeper opening preparation, complex endgames and game analysis.

  • Extending and deepening your opening repertoire
  • Complex and theoretical endgames
  • Analysing your tournament games in depth
  • Time management in competitive games
Level 07

Master Level

Advanced strategy and serious tournament preparation.

  • Studying classic master games
  • Preparing for specific opponents and events
  • Dynamic play and converting advantages
  • Training plans for rated tournaments
Level 08

Grand Master Level

Elite-level training for players aiming at titles.

  • Deep opening preparation
  • High-level strategic and endgame technique
  • Competitive and psychological preparation
  • Individual training plans with the coach

Class types

Small batches or one-on-one.

Small batch classes

Learn with a small group of players at the same level. In person in Hyderabad, or live online.

One-on-one classes

Your own FIDE-rated tutor and a plan built around you. In person in Hyderabad, or live online.

Ages 6 and up. First class free, no registration fee. Students in the USA learn online.
