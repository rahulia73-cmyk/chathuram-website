Questions

Before you start

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your first class is a free trial, and there is no registration fee.

What age can students start?

Students can start from 5 years old. Adults are welcome at every level.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the class type (small batch or one-on-one) and the level. Message us on WhatsApp at @@whatsapp, or call @@phoneIndia in India, for current fees.

Which level should I join?

Tell us about the student's chess experience and we'll recommend the right level from 01 to 08.

Do you have in-person classes in the USA?

No. In-person classes are in Hyderabad only. Students in the USA, including Chicago, join our live online classes.

What do I need for online classes?

A laptop or tablet with a stable internet connection. We'll share the class link before the first session.
