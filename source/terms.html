Classes and the free trial

We offer small batch and one-on-one classes, in person in Hyderabad and online. Students in the USA learn online. Your first class is a free trial, and there is no registration fee.

Class times, the student's level, and fees are agreed with you directly before regular classes begin.

Fees

Fees depend on the class type and level, and are shared on request. The fees and payment arrangements we confirm with you are the ones that apply.

Students under 18

A parent or guardian should contact us and agree to classes on behalf of a student under 18.

Online classes

Online students need a laptop or tablet and a stable internet connection. Class links are for enrolled students only and should not be shared.

Conduct

We expect every student to play fairly and respect their tutor and opponents. Like in chess, we start and finish with a handshake.
