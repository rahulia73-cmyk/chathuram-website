What chess builds

Six skills, one board.

Chess is a game anyone can learn, but it rewards the habits that help in school, at work and in life.

Focus

A game of chess asks for full attention. Students learn to stay with one problem for longer, without getting distracted.

Problem solving

Every position is a puzzle. Students learn to look for options, compare them and choose the best one.

Planning ahead

"If I go here, what happens next?" Chess builds the habit of thinking a few steps ahead before acting.

Patience

Good moves come from taking your time. Chess rewards slowing down and checking your idea before you play it.

Learning from mistakes

Every lost game is a lesson. Reviewing games teaches students to learn from mistakes and try again.

Confidence & sportsmanship

Win with grace, lose with a handshake. Students grow more confident with every game, and learn to respect their opponent.

For every age

A game for life.

Children can start from 6 years old, and adults are welcome at every level. Chess needs no special fitness or equipment, and it can be played anywhere, across the table or online.

Like a pawn, every student starts small. With every square they move forward, they can become more.
