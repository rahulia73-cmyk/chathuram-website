What this website collects

This website has no accounts, no logins and no database. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information.

The enquiry form on our Contact page does not send anything to us by itself. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing is sent until you choose to send the message there.

What you share with us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, you may share details such as your name, phone number, city, the student's age and their chess level. We use these only to reply to you, recommend a level, and arrange and run classes. We keep them only as long as we need them for that.

Children

Many of our students are children. We ask that a parent or guardian contacts us on their behalf. Photos or videos of students only appear on this website with a parent's or guardian's permission, and we will remove any of them on request.

Other services this website uses

Some parts of this website are provided by other companies, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the text font.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us.
  • YouTube, if a video is shown on the website. It is embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Amazon Kindle, if you follow a link to our puzzle book.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Your choices
